## 1. Purpose

These terms establish the general framework for the commercial licensing and distribution of LibraryAI digital books through authorized business-to-business partners.

LibraryAI enables commercial partners to select approved digital books and offer them through their own e-commerce channels.

These terms do not, by themselves, activate a commercial partnership. Partner-specific commercial conditions must be separately agreed.

## 3. Commercial License

Subject to an effective commercial agreement, the Licensor may grant a partner a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to distribute selected LibraryAI books.

The authorization may be restricted to:

- Approved book titles.
- Authorized e-commerce channels.
- Agreed commercial activities.
- The applicable contractual period.
- Any expressly agreed territories.

No ownership of source code or underlying intellectual property is transferred.

## 4. Book Catalog Selection

Partners may select the titles they wish to distribute from the available LibraryAI catalog.

Partners are not required to distribute the entire catalog unless separately agreed.

Each title may have specific licensing conditions and commercial fees.

Catalog updates remain subject to existing contractual and customer obligations.

## 5. Retail Sales and Payment Collection

Where a partner acts as the seller, it receives payments directly from its customers.

Eligible sales must be registered through LibraryAI or an approved integration.

Transaction records should include:

- Book identification.
- Transaction reference.
- Sales channel.
- Sale date.
- Eligible sale value.
- Payment confirmation.
- Refund or cancellation status.

## 6. Book Licensing Fees

Partners shall pay the book licensing fees agreed with O Mundo do Leo Unipessoal, Lda.

The applicable fees and calculation methods must be defined in the partner's commercial conditions.

These conditions shall address discounts, taxes, refunds, and adjustments.

Retail pricing arrangements must comply with applicable competition law.

Fixed or minimum resale prices may only be imposed where legally permissible.

## 7. Artificial Intelligence Charges

O Mundo do Leo Unipessoal, Lda. operates the AI infrastructure supporting LibraryAI.

Partners are responsible for eligible AI consumption associated with their operations.

```
AI Charge = Eligible AI Cost × 1.15
```

The additional 15% represents an infrastructure management fee payable to O Mundo do Leo Unipessoal, Lda.

AI charges are separate from book licensing fees.

## 8. Prepaid AI Balances

LibraryAI may require partners to maintain prepaid balances for AI consumption.

AI-powered operations may be restricted when the available balance is insufficient.

Balance management, refunds, and usage conditions shall be defined in the applicable commercial agreement.
