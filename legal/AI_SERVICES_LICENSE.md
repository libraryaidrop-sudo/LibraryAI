## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the licensing principles governing artificial intelligence services provided through LibraryAI to authorized B2B partners.

Services may include:

- Interactive narrative generation.
- Personalized storytelling.
- Character customization.
- AI-assisted illustrations.
- Voice narration and audio generation.
- Narrative continuity management.
- Automated editorial validation.
- Quality control workflows.

## 2. Infrastructure Ownership and Operation

O Mundo do Leo Unipessoal, Lda. operates the AI infrastructure supporting LibraryAI.

The company maintains the relevant provider accounts, integrations, and contractual relationships and initially pays the associated provider charges.

Development, configuration, technical management, and quality control are performed on behalf of the company.

## 3. Authorized B2B Access

Commercial partners may access AI functionality exclusively through approved LibraryAI interfaces and integrations.

Access is limited to the services included in the applicable commercial agreement.

Partners do not acquire ownership of proprietary prompts, AI assistant configurations, workflows, or underlying software.

## 4. AI Consumption Charges

Each authorized B2B partner is responsible for eligible AI consumption generated through its operations.

```
Partner AI Charge = Eligible AI Cost × 1.15
```

The additional 15% represents an AI infrastructure management fee retained by O Mundo do Leo Unipessoal, Lda.

This charge is separate from book licensing fees and excludes applicable taxes unless otherwise agreed.

## 5. Eligible Costs

Eligible AI costs may include:

- Text and narrative generation.
- Image and illustration generation.
- Voice narration.
- AI-based editorial validation.
- Other AI operations expressly included in the commercial agreement.

Cost allocation methods, billing units, currency conversion, and rounding rules shall be defined in the applicable commercial terms.

The same underlying provider cost shall not be charged twice.

## 6. Consumption Monitoring

LibraryAI may record:

- Eligible provider cost.

Partners shall receive access to relevant consumption and billing information.
